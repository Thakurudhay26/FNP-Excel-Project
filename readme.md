# 🌸 FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

A dynamic and interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product popularity, and occasion-based revenue trends for Ferns and Petals (FNP).

## 📌 Short Description / Purpose

The FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard is an Excel-based business intelligence project developed to uncover meaningful insights from Ferns and Petals sales data. The dashboard helps stakeholders understand revenue generation, customer purchasing behavior, delivery performance, product demand, and sales trends across various occasions such as Diwali, Holi, Raksha Bandhan, Birthdays, Anniversaries, and Valentine's Day.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 **Microsoft Excel** – Primary platform used for data analysis and dashboard development.
- 📈 **Pivot Tables** – Used for data summarization and business insight generation.
- 📉 **Pivot Charts** – Created interactive visualizations for trend and performance analysis.
- 🎛️ **Slicers & Filters** – Enabled dynamic dashboard interaction and data exploration.
- 🧹 **Data Cleaning & Transformation** – Performed using Excel functions and formatting techniques.
- 📁 **File Format** – `.xlsx` for dashboard development and analysis.

---

## 📂 Data Source

- **Source:** Ferns and Petals (FNP) Sales Dataset.
- **Fields Included:** Customer Information, Orders, Products, Revenue, Quantity, Order Date, Delivery Date, Occasion, City, and Product Categories.

---

## ✨ Features / Highlights

### 🔹 Business Problem

Ferns and Petals serves customers across multiple cities and occasions throughout the year. With growing sales volumes and diverse product offerings, identifying customer preferences, top-performing products, delivery efficiency, and revenue trends becomes challenging without proper data analysis.

### 🔹 Goal of the Dashboard

To provide a centralized analytical dashboard that:

- Tracks overall revenue and sales performance.
- Understands customer spending behavior.
- Measures delivery efficiency and timelines.
- Identifies top-performing products.
- Compares revenue generated across different occasions.
- Supports data-driven business decisions.

---

## 📊 Key Business Questions Answered

1. **Total Revenue** – Identify the overall revenue generated.
2. **Average Order and Delivery Time** – Evaluate the time taken for orders to be delivered.
3. **Monthly Sales Performance** – Analyze sales fluctuations throughout 2023.
4. **Top Products by Revenue** – Determine the highest revenue-generating products.
5. **Customer Spending Analysis** – Understand average customer spending behavior.
6. **Sales Performance by Top 5 Products** – Track the performance of top-selling products.
7. **Top 10 Cities by Number of Orders** – Identify cities with the highest order volumes.
8. **Order Quantity vs. Delivery Time** – Analyze the impact of order quantity on delivery time.
9. **Revenue Comparison Between Occasions** – Compare revenue generated across occasions.
10. **Product Popularity by Occasion** – Determine the most popular products during specific occasions.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

- Identified top-performing products contributing the highest revenue.
- Analyzed monthly sales trends to detect peak business periods.
- Compared revenue across major gifting occasions.
- Evaluated customer spending patterns and purchase behavior.
- Highlighted top-performing cities based on order volume.
- Measured delivery performance to improve operational efficiency.
